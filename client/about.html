What is the WorkEpic Index and how does it work?

The WorkEpic Index is a self assessment tool designed to determine your unique classification based on your honest responses to a series of questions. Upon completing the test, you'll receive a detailed report and your WorkEpic Index score for a fixed fee of R99.

This psychometric assessment has been developed with care and is intended to provide insights into personal preferences, strengths, behavioural tendencies, and potential areas of development. While every effort has been made to ensure the quality and reliability of the assessment, results should be interpreted as guidance rather than definitive conclusions.

Participants are welcome to include their assessment outcomes on their CV, LinkedIn profile, or other professional platforms, provided the results are represented accurately and in their proper context.

Your responses and results are treated as confidential and will be handled in accordance with applicable privacy standards. Assessment outcomes are intended for personal development, self awareness, and professional growth purposes only. By completing this assessment, you acknowledge that the results are informational in nature and do not constitute psychological, medical, legal, educational, or career counselling advice.
